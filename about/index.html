About Krishi Sahayak

Empowering Farmers with AI-Driven Agricultural Intelligence

Our Mission

At Krishi Sahayak, our mission is to make advanced agricultural technology accessible to every farmer, regardless of their location, native language, or farm size. We believe that integrating human farming intuition with artificial intelligence creates sustainable and high-yielding agricultural practices.

What We Do

Krishi Sahayak is a state-of-the-art AI assistant designed directly for the mobile devices farmers use every day. We provide an ecosystem supporting:

  • Precision Diagnostics: Capture images of diseased crops, and our vision model identifies the pathogen and recommends organic and chemical treatments.
  • Multilingual Support: Powered by the Bhashini API, we converse in local Indian languages so farmers can operate in their mother tongue effortlessly.
  • Market & Weather Intelligence: Delivering accurate weather projections and live mandi prices to inform sowing and selling strategies.

Our Team

Built by technologists with roots in agriculture. We actively collaborate with agronomists, local farming communities, and AI researchers to refine our models and create a platform that stands as a true automated helper—a "Sahayak"—in the field.
